Certified Project Management Associate (CPMA)

A foundation-level project management course for professionals who need practical project planning, coordination and delivery skills.

Course overview

Build a practical foundation in project delivery.

This associate course introduces the core tools used to define, plan, coordinate and report project work in professional environments.

  • Understand the project life cycle and the role of a project team member
  • Define project scope, deliverables, stakeholders and success criteria
  • Build basic schedules, work plans and responsibility charts
  • Use simple risk, communication and progress reporting tools

Modules

  • 01
    Project foundations
    Project terms, roles, life cycle stages and delivery expectations.
  • 02
    Scope and work planning
    Objectives, deliverables, work breakdown and basic scheduling.
  • 03
    Resources and coordination
    Task owners, timelines, dependencies and team communication.
  • 04
    Risk and reporting
    Issue tracking, risk registers, progress updates and escalation.
  • 05
    Assessment and certification
    Practical review, quiz and certificate eligibility.
