Application requirements

What applicants should prepare before review.

Basic documents

Updated CV or professional profile, passport photograph, valid means of identification and highest academic qualification or student/NYSC evidence where applicable.

Experience evidence

Work experience, certification, competence, business practice, leadership role, consulting work or professional contribution may support category review.

Category recommendation

IIPM reviews the applicant profile, recommends the right category, then confirms payment and onboarding steps after approval.

CPD and active status

Renewal, learning and verification rules.

Recommended annual CPD points

  • Student / Graduate: 10 points
  • Associate: 15 points
  • Full Member: 20 points
  • Fellow: 25 points

Membership verification

  • Each admitted member should have a unique membership number and official record.
  • Active members should be verifiable through the membership verification platform or directory.
  • Unrenewed membership may be marked inactive until renewal or reinstatement.

Renewal options

Renewal fees are handled after admission and active-status review. Life membership options apply to Associate, Full Member and Fellow categories.
