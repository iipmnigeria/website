Weekend Executive Certification Programme

Certified Management Consultant

A management consulting course for professionals who advise organizations, diagnose performance gaps and present practical recommendations.

Course overview

Develop consulting discipline, diagnosis and advisory confidence.

This course introduces consulting methods for client discovery, problem diagnosis, analysis, recommendation and implementation support.

  • Structure consulting engagements from discovery to recommendation
  • Use diagnostic tools to identify organizational performance gaps
  • Prepare clear reports, presentations and advisory outputs
  • Manage client communication, scope and implementation support

Modules

  • 01
    Consulting foundations
    Engagement types, ethics, scope and consultant-client roles.
  • 02
    Problem diagnosis
    Discovery questions, data gathering, interviews and root causes.
  • 03
    Analysis and insight
    Frameworks, evidence, options and decision criteria.
  • 04
    Recommendations
    Reports, presentations, roadmaps and implementation support.
  • 05
    Assessment and certification
    Consulting case project and certificate eligibility.
