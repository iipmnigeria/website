Weekend Executive Certification Programme

Certified Generative Artificial Intelligence Course

A weekend executive course for professionals who want to use generative AI tools responsibly for productivity, analysis and workplace innovation.

Course overview

Use generative AI responsibly in professional work.

This course introduces practical AI workflows, prompt design and governance considerations for professionals and organizations.

  • Understand generative AI concepts, capabilities and limits
  • Create better prompts for research, writing, analysis and productivity
  • Apply AI tools to everyday management and operational tasks
  • Identify ethical, privacy and quality risks in AI-assisted work

Modules

  • 01
    AI foundations
    Core concepts, model capabilities, limitations and use cases.
  • 02
    Prompting practice
    Clear instructions, context design, iteration and evaluation.
  • 03
    Productivity workflows
    Documents, research, planning, analysis and team support.
  • 04
    Governance and ethics
    Privacy, bias, verification, policy and responsible adoption.
  • 05
    Capstone and certification
    Practical AI workflow project and certificate eligibility.
