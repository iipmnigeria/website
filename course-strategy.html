Course overview

Build execution discipline and measurable performance.

This programme is designed for managers, team leads, consultants, entrepreneurs and professionals responsible for strategy, operations or performance improvement.

  • Understand strategic planning and execution frameworks
  • Convert goals into measurable performance dashboards
  • Improve team productivity and accountability systems
  • Complete assessments required for certification

Modules

  • 01
    Strategy foundations
    Vision, priorities, operating context and performance gaps.
  • 02
    Execution planning
    Roadmaps, owners, milestones, scorecards and review rhythm.
  • 03
    Productivity systems
    Workflow design, resource alignment and team accountability.
  • 04
    Change and adoption
    Communication, stakeholder management and risk reduction.
  • 05
    Assessment and certification
    Quiz, practical case review and certificate eligibility.
