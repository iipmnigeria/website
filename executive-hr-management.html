Course title

Executive Professional Programme in Strategic Human Resource Management, Administration and Performance.

Advance from foundation to professional level through one structured 8-weekend executive programme.

  • Human Resource Management Foundation Certified (HRMFC)
  • Certified Human Resource Management Generalist (CHRMG)
  • Certified Human Resource Management Professional (CHRMP)
  • Executive Professional Certificate in Strategic Human Resource Management, Administration and Performance

Standards-informed by

SHRM, CIPM, HRCI and CIPD.

Ideal for

HR officers, administrators, managers, supervisors, business owners, consultants, school administrators, NGO officers and professionals responsible for people, performance, administration and workplace productivity.

May support eligible participants pursuing CIPM Membership and the CIPM Licensed HR Practitioner pathway, subject to applicable requirements and approval.
