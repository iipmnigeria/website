Certified Human Resource Management Generalist (CHRMG)

A practical HR generalist course for professionals handling people operations, employee records and workplace administration.

Course overview

Build strong HR administration and people-management foundations.

This course covers the essential HR responsibilities needed to support recruitment, onboarding, employee records and workplace performance.

  • Understand the role of HR in organizational performance
  • Support recruitment, onboarding and employee documentation
  • Maintain basic HR records, policies and compliance routines
  • Assist managers with performance, discipline and employee relations processes

Modules

  • 01
    HR function essentials
    The HR role, policy environment and employee life cycle.
  • 02
    Recruitment and onboarding
    Job profiles, screening, induction and probation support.
  • 03
    Employee records
    Documentation, personnel files, leave records and HR reporting.
  • 04
    Performance support
    Basic appraisal, discipline, grievance and engagement routines.
  • 05
    Assessment and certification
    Practical review, quiz and certificate eligibility.
