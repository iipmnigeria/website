Foundation Course

Project Management Foundation Certified (PMFC)

A foundation-level project management course for learners starting with the basic language, structure and responsibilities of project work.

Course overview

Start with the fundamentals of project management.

This foundation course introduces the essential concepts, documents and coordination routines used in project environments.

  • Understand common project management terms, roles and responsibilities
  • Identify project objectives, stakeholders, deliverables and constraints
  • Recognize the purpose of project schedules, budgets, risks and reports
  • Prepare for progression into associate and professional project management levels

Modules

  • 01
    Project basics
    Project meaning, life cycle stages, roles and responsibilities.
  • 02
    Project initiation
    Objectives, stakeholders, deliverables, constraints and assumptions.
  • 03
    Planning essentials
    Tasks, timelines, resources, basic budgets and responsibilities.
  • 04
    Delivery awareness
    Communication, risks, issues, reporting and closeout basics.
  • 05
    Assessment and certification
    Foundation quiz, practical exercise and certificate eligibility.
