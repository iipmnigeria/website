Certified Emotional Intelligence Associate

An associate emotional intelligence course for professionals improving self-awareness, communication and workplace relationships.

Course overview

Build self-awareness and stronger professional relationships.

This course introduces emotional intelligence concepts that support communication, teamwork, conflict handling and personal effectiveness.

  • Recognize emotions, triggers and behavioral patterns
  • Improve communication, empathy and active listening
  • Manage workplace pressure and interpersonal tension
  • Apply emotional intelligence to team collaboration

Modules

  • 01
    Self-awareness
    Personal triggers, strengths, blind spots and emotional patterns.
  • 02
    Self-management
    Stress handling, resilience and professional composure.
  • 03
    Social awareness
    Empathy, listening, inclusion and workplace signals.
  • 04
    Relationship management
    Communication, feedback, conflict and collaboration.
  • 05
    Assessment and certification
    Reflection exercise, quiz and certificate eligibility.
