Certified Business Analysis Professional

A professional business analysis course for practitioners leading requirements, process improvement and solution evaluation work.

Course overview

Advance business analysis for complex change and solution delivery.

This course strengthens analysis capability for professionals working across business change, systems, process and performance improvement.

  • Lead structured requirements and stakeholder analysis
  • Analyze business processes, gaps, risks and solution options
  • Evaluate solution fit, benefits and implementation readiness
  • Prepare professional business analysis reports and decision support

Modules

  • 01
    Enterprise analysis
    Business needs, goals, context, constraints and opportunity framing.
  • 02
    Advanced requirements
    Prioritization, traceability, validation and change control.
  • 03
    Process improvement
    Gap analysis, root causes, redesign and performance measures.
  • 04
    Solution evaluation
    Options, benefits, risks, readiness and adoption support.
  • 05
    Assessment and certification
    Case assignment, quiz and certificate eligibility.
