Weekend Executive Certification Programme

Certified Professional in Cybersecurity

A weekend executive cybersecurity course for professionals who need practical awareness of digital risk, controls and safe workplace behavior.

Course overview

Build practical cybersecurity awareness and risk control skills.

This course helps professionals understand common threats, protect information and support safer digital operations in the workplace.

  • Identify common cybersecurity threats and workplace vulnerabilities
  • Apply safe password, identity and access management practices
  • Recognize phishing, social engineering and data exposure risks
  • Support incident reporting and basic cyber risk controls

Modules

  • 01
    Cybersecurity foundations
    Threat landscape, risk concepts and business impact.
  • 02
    Identity and access
    Passwords, MFA, privileges, device hygiene and user behavior.
  • 03
    Threat awareness
    Phishing, malware, social engineering and unsafe workflows.
  • 04
    Data protection
    Confidentiality, privacy, backups and secure information handling.
  • 05
    Assessment and certification
    Scenario review, quiz and certificate eligibility.
